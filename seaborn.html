Seaborn: The Beautiful Painter

What is it?

Seaborn takes your boring data and automatically turns it into stunning, colorful graphs. It does all the hard work of picking good colors and styles so your charts look professional instantly.

How to use it

import seaborn as sns
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Let's draw a scatter plot from some data!
sns.scatterplot(x="Age", y="Salary", data=my_data)
plt.show()

Line 1 & 2: We load Seaborn (the painter) and Matplotlib (the canvas).

Line 5: We tell Seaborn to draw dots (scatter plot). The dots represent Age (left to right) and Salary (up and down).

Line 6: We tell the canvas to show our beautiful painting to the screen.

Real-World Magic

Discovering Trends: By plotting customer ages against what they buy, a business can visually see patterns (like younger people buying more tech) and target their ads perfectly.
